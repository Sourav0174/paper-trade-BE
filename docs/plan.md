# PaperTrade V2 — 7-Phase Roadmap

PaperTrade V2 is planned across 7 major phases:

### Phase 1 — AI Foundation & Core Hardening

- Fix current AI Mentor issues
- Cache-first Mentor experience
- Deterministic fallback system
- Multi-provider AI architecture
- Improve AI infrastructure and reliability

### Phase 2 — App-Wide AI Coaching

- Pre-trade risk checks
- Post-trade execution debriefs
- Stock and market intelligence
- Reusable AI components across the app

### Phase 3 — AI Trade Journal & Portfolio Intelligence

- AI-powered trade journal
- Weekly performance reports
- Trading habit tracking
- Conversational AI Trading Coach
- Deeper portfolio intelligence

### Phase 4 — Options Infrastructure

- NIFTY/BANK NIFTY options infrastructure
- Option contracts and expiry management
- Black-Scholes-Merton (BSM) pricing
- Greeks calculation
- Synthetic option chain

### Phase 5 — Options Paper Trading

- Long CE/PE paper trading
- Options order execution
- Options positions and P&L
- Square-off functionality
- Expiry settlement

### Phase 6 — AI + Options

- AI explanations for Greeks
- Options-specific risk checks
- Options behavioral coaching
- AI-powered options trade insights

### Phase 7 — Hardening & Production Release

- Portfolio snapshots
- Comprehensive testing
- Performance benchmarking
- Error resilience and graceful fallbacks
- Compliance and disclosures
- Final production polish

## Overall Direction

**Fix AI foundation → make AI available across the app → build intelligent journaling and coaching → build the options engine → enable options paper trading → combine AI with options → harden and release.**
